About the Event

This website is inspired by Quincy Larson, the founder of FreeCodeCamp. In his autobiography he mentions how attending networking events is what created his careeer.

Networking is often viewed as intimidating, and the events themself may seem like they hardly happen, which makes it easy to avoid doing so, but that couldn't be farther from the truth!

The goal of this website is to incentivise networking by gamifying it! Here's how to get started!

What to Expect

Do you want to become a coding wizard? Then join us!

  • Code alongside fellow aspiring or current software engineers in your community.
  • Collab during hackathons, meetings or to create your own startups!
  • Connect and network to create friends that can be utilized for your future career.
  • Community engagement!
